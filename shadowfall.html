Gather resources by clicking them, craft weapons and tonics, and build a shelter before the waves reach you. While inside your shelter, regenerate 3 health per second and deal 50% more damage. Move with WASD, jump with W, and press SPACE for melee attacks. Craft and equip the Warden Bow with 5 wood and 3 iron, then face an enemy and click in the play area to shoot.
